Payment & house rules

Cash is accepted. Bank transfer on arrival is available only for phone bookings paid at check-in or online plans that allow payment on the day of arrival / at check-in. Other plans follow the booking platform’s terms.

  • Children are welcome; no cots or extra beds.
  • No smoking, pets or parties.
  • Please keep noise low from 22:00 to 08:00.
Prices & cancellation

Prices vary by date and room. Please call or check the booking platforms. Confirm cancellation terms at the time of booking; platform bookings follow the selected plan and confirmation.

Families & whole-house stays

Families and children are welcome. Please call with your dates, number of guests and children’s ages so we can confirm a suitable arrangement. Quiet hours and the no-party rule still apply.

Parking, luggage & late arrival

There are ample outdoor spaces in front of the homestay, and parking is also possible under the covered entrance, subject to conditions on arrival. Please confirm luggage-storage times. If you may arrive after 22:00, call ahead to see whether an arrangement is possible.

Bathroom & hot springs

Every room has a private bathroom; no bidet toilet. We do not have a spa. Zhiben hot spring operators, including Dongyouji, are around 15–25 minutes away by car depending on traffic; check their admission and hours directly.

Breakfast, early check-in & late check-out

Whether breakfast is included depends on your booking plan; please check it when booking or ask us by phone. Early check-in and late check-out depend on availability and cannot be guaranteed. Please call ahead.
